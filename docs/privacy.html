Privacy policy

Flip Focus · Last updated 17 October 2026

The short version

Flip Focus does not collect anything. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no network access at all. The app never connects to the internet.

What is stored and where

The only data the app writes is your own settings: session lengths, window size, language and similar preferences. They are saved in a single file on your computer:

%APPDATA%\FlipFocus\settings.json

That file never leaves your computer. You can open it, edit it or delete it whenever you want. Deleting it simply resets the app to its defaults.

Network

The app makes no network requests. It contains no telemetry library, no update checker and no remote content. The quotes, the chime and the icons are all built into the program itself.

Third parties

None. No data is shared with anyone, because no data is collected.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, children included.

Microsooft Store version

When you install the app from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft processes the installation and its own store telemetry under its own privacy policy. The app itself still collects nothing.

Changes

If this ever changes, the new version of this page will say so and the application will ask you before sending anything.

Contact

Questions or issues: github.com/otamendez11/flip-focus/issues

Privacidad

Flip Focus no recoge nada. No hay cuenta, ni estadísticas, ni publicidad, ni informes de errores, ni acceso a internet. La aplicación nunca se conecta.

Lo único que guarda son tus propios ajustes, en un archivo de tu ordenador: %APPDATA%\FlipFocus\settings.json. Ese archivo no sale de tu equipo y puedes borrarlo cuando quieras; al borrarlo la app vuelve a sus valores por defecto.
